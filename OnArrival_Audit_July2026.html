Prepared July 14, 2026 · Method: live page fetch + web-search citation footprint · Scope: onarrival.com
Bottom line. OnArrival has good bones — a clean URL structure, hand-written meta descriptions, real customer logos and a credible funding/press footprint. But the site is visibly unfinished: multiple live product pages ship with Lorem-ipsum placeholder text, testimonials are dummy quotes attributed to real named CEOs, and the index is polluted with leftover pages from an old consumer-travel template. The result is a site that AI engines and Google can reach but can't confidently describe or recommend. On the highest-value query — "best embedded travel API platform" — OnArrival is completely absent while Duffel, Nuitée, Amadeus and others own the space.

⚠️ Measurement note: The live browser extension was unavailable during this run, so Core Web Vitals timings and JSON-LD schema were assessed from fetched HTML rather than a live crawl, and backlink volumes are directional (no Ahrefs/Semrush connection). Items marked (verify live) should be confirmed with a crawler before implementation sign-off.

1 · What OnArrival is doing well

SEO strengths

  • Unique, hand-written meta descriptions on every page checked — keyword-relevant and human, not auto-generated. This is better than most seed-stage sites.
  • Clean, semantic URL architecture — /solutions/core-travel/flights, /usecases/embedded-travel. Logical hierarchy that both crawlers and users can parse.
  • Full Open Graph + Twitter Card tags on all pages — good link previews in Slack, LinkedIn, iMessage and AI chat surfaces.
  • HTTPS, mobile viewport meta, responsive Webflow build — technical baseline is met.
  • Strong social proof assets — real customer/partner logos (Cleartrip, Niyo Global, Jupiter, TATA AIG, Zolve, dnata, Holiday Tribe). Excellent raw material once the copy around them is finished.

GEO / AI-citation strengths

  • Solid brand-query footprint. On a direct "tell me about OnArrival" query, AI engines have plenty to draw on: Tracxn, Crunchbase, PitchBook, Dealroom, CB Insights company profiles plus a feature in Skift (Dec 2025) — a genuinely authoritative travel-industry citation source.
  • Consistent entity naming — "OnArrival" / "OnArrival Travel Technology Pvt Ltd" is used coherently across directories, which helps AI resolve the entity.
  • Clear positioning language ("travel-as-a-service", "embedded travel infrastructure") that maps to how AI engines cluster the category.

Backlink strengths

  • Credible early referring domains for a 2022 seed company: Skift, BW Disrupt, YourStory, Startup Story, plus investor pages (Antler blog: "Investing in OnArrival").
  • Investor-driven links from Antler, Good Capital and Inuka VC pages carry topical authority.
