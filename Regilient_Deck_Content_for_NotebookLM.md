## Slide 2 — How to read this report
**Kicker:** Credibility first
**Intro:** A fact-based audit, not a sales flyer. Every finding carries a confidence label and a one-line way to check it yourself. No invented numbers.

Four confidence tiers:
- **MEASURED** — We observed it directly on the live page or search result. Quote or screenshot provided.
- **BENCHMARK** — Comparison against competitors, drawn from a named public source.
- **ESTIMATED** — Directional; method stated. Confirm with the named live tool.
- **RECOMMENDATION** — Our professional opinion or proposed fix.

**Footnote:** Tools this pass: live page fetch, Google search (site: + category queries), public software-review sources. ESTIMATED items are confirmed with a live crawler / Ahrefs in a follow-up.
