## Non-negotiable principles
- **Every claim must be checkable.** For each finding include a confidence label — **MEASURED** (observed live), **BENCHMARK** (vs competitors from a named source), **ESTIMATED** (directional; name the tool to confirm), **RECOMMENDATION** (opinion). Do not invent metrics.
- **Be honest that Qoruz is strong at classic SEO.** The pitch is NOT "your SEO is broken." It is "you win Google SEO in India; the open frontier is AI-answer visibility (GEO) + entity clarity, and Quilzy is the engine for that."
- **No guaranteed rankings.** Quilzy's promise = it removes the technical reasons content fails to get found and reports **impressions + AI-citation share** as headline metrics.
- Keep the roadmap light; lead the back half with **outcome → how → differentiator → proof.**

## Design system
- Palette: deep navy `#0B1220` (title/closing slides + dark stat cards), deep blue `#0C2A4A`, cyan accent `#06B6D4` / darker cyan `#0E7490`, light panels `#F1F5F9`, ink `#0F172A`, muted `#64748B`. Severity: red `#DC2626` (P0), orange `#EA580C` (P1), amber `#B45309` (P2), green `#16A34A` (pass/MEASURED).
- Fonts: serif headers (Cambria/Georgia), sans body (Calibri/Arial). Titles 30–44pt bold, body 12–15pt.
- Motif: rounded cards, icon-in-colored-circle, big-number stat callouts. **No accent stripes/underlines under titles.** Dark cover + dark proof + dark CTA; light content slides. Confidential footer on every slide: `Quilzy · SEO/GEO Visibility Audit · Confidential   |   Qoruz · <date>`.
